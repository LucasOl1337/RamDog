# Code of Conduct

RamDog follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good faith, give and accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the people using and building RamDog. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing other people's private information are not acceptable, in English, Portuguese or any other language.

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior makes you uncomfortable, report it privately to the maintainer through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/LucasOl1337/RamDog/security/advisories/new) (use it for conduct reports too) or by contacting [@LucasOl1337](https://github.com/LucasOl1337) on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions and restrict participation for anyone who does not follow this code.
